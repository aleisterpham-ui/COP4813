Research Summary

Each CMS has different strengths and limitations. WordPress is popular for general websites, Drupal and TYPO3 provide advanced content management features, Joomla offers flexible website development, and Ghost focuses on publishing and memberships.

The best CMS depends on the website's purpose, technical requirements, available resources, and long-term maintenance needs.
